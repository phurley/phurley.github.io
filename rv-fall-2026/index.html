Sue's weekday strength

Excellent: Asheville, Chattanooga, Cincinnati/Lebanon.

Very good: Yellow Springs, Lexington.

Good with planning: Hocking Hills.

Question mark: New River Gorge. Fayetteville is pleasant but small; Tamarack and Bridge Walk help. If any base gets cut after review, this is the first one to reconsider.

RV grades & road stress

Easy: Canton → Yellow Springs; Lexington → Lebanon → home.

Moderate: Yellow Springs → Hocking; Hocking → Fayetteville; Asheville → Chattanooga; Chattanooga → Lexington.

Most serious: Fayetteville → Asheville. Keep the coach on major routes, travel in daylight, and don't make it a must-be-online-by-11 day.

Blue Ridge Parkway: use the Escape for scenic Parkway drives. Recheck current NPS closures shortly before the trip.
